**Date:** 2026-06-20

## What was done

Added `setupNavScroll()` method to the Component class in `Portfolio.dc.html`.

The method intercepts clicks on all internal anchor links (`a[href^="#"]`), prevents the native browser anchor behavior (which was not correctly applying the nav offset), and manually smooth-scrolls to the target section with the exact nav height subtracted plus a 10px buffer.

Special-cased `#top` (logo link) to scroll to position 0.
