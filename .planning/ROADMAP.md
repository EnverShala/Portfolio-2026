### Phase 2: Content & Functionality
**Goal**: Make the portfolio fully functional — real links, real images, working contact form, SEO basics, language persistence.
**Mode**: mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 1
**Requirements**: CNT-01, CNT-02, CNT-03, CNT-04, CNT-05, FNC-01, FNC-02
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. All six project buttons (Live-Test + Github) navigate to real URLs
  2. GitHub and LinkedIn social icons open real profile pages
  3. All three project cards display actual screenshot images
  4. Contact form delivers submissions to user's email inbox
  5. Language preference (DE/EN) survives page reload

### Phase 3: Legal Compliance
**Goal**: Add Impressum and privacy policy — required by German law and GDPR for a professional services site.

**Depends on**: Phase 2
**Requirements**: LEG-01, LEG-02
